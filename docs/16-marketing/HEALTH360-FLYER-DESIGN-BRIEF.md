---

## 1. Purpose of this flyer

Create one **corporate-quality, single-page flyer** that a hospital leader, clinician, or conference visitor can understand in **under 10 seconds**.

It must feel like an **enterprise healthcare technology product**, not a generic medical advertisement.

**Primary job:** Communicate what Health360 is, who it serves, what it connects, showcase the platform feature set, and drive **EXPLORE HEALTH360 / SCAN THE QR CODE**.

---

## 2. What is Health360?

Health360 is TechEarnest’s **complete digital healthcare / hospital management platform**. It connects the full healthcare journey on **one unified digital platform** — patients, doctors, hospitals, laboratories, pharmacies, and hospital operations — around a single longitudinal patient record and an encounter-centric clinical hub.

**Taglines (mandatory hierarchy):**
1. **HEALTH360** (hero product)
2. **Experience Digital Care**
3. **A Healthcare Product by TechEarnest**
4. Company line (optional, secondary): **Empowering Possibilities**

## 7. Health360 features (full platform catalog)

Present these as **our features**. Grouped for designer selection — not for status labeling.

### 7.1 Patient identity & access
- User authentication and secure login  
- Patient profile linked 1:1 with user identity  
- **UHID** — lifelong healthcare identifier  
- Health profile  
- Duplicate detection / find-or-register at desk  
- Emergency / temporary registration  
- Patient portal (web + mobile baseline)  

### 7.2 Hospital & doctor discovery
- Hospital organization & profile  
- Doctor search and discovery  
- Doctor–hospital association  
- Doctor schedules  

### 7.3 Appointments & front desk
- Patient appointment booking  
- Desk booking and slot management  
- Appointment lifecycle (booked → confirmed → arrived → completed / cancelled / no-show / rescheduled)  
- Desk patient search (UHID / mobile / name)  
- Reception registration + credentials  
- Walk-in registration  
- Desk arrive / check-in  
- Self check-in (app / QR)  

### 7.4 OPD & queue
- Digital OPD queue  
- Token generation  
- Call / skip / recall / start / complete  
- Live queue status for desk, doctor, and patient  
- Patient live OPD tracking page  
- Queue ↔ encounter ↔ appointment status sync  

### 7.5 Clinical workflows
- Encounter-centric clinical hub  
- Encounter-scoped vitals  
- Structured consultation / clinical notes  
- Complaint & symptoms (catalog-supported)  
- Diagnosis / ICD catalogs  
- Guided visit checklist  
- Wellness plans  
- Clinical follow-up  
- Today’s consultation summary  
- Patient clinical timeline  
- E-prescription (draft → signed)  
- Prescription safety checks  
- Checkout gate (consult + Rx completeness)  

### 7.6 Laboratory
- Lab order from clinical workflow  
- Hospital lab booking  
- Sample collection  
- Results draft → verify → release / publish report  
- Patient lab report access  
- Lab report-ready notifications  
- Structured lab values & metrics  
- Lab / radiology hardening (barcode, alerts, billing gates)  
- Radiology module workflows  

### 7.7 Pharmacy
- Clinical pharmacy / medication orders  
- Inpatient MAR (medication administration)  
- MAR mobile  
- Pharmacy inventory & batches  
- Pharmacy dispensing rules & returns  
- E-prescription share to pharmacy  
- Pharmacy request → review → dispense journey  
- Independent pharmacy organization support  

### 7.8 IPD · ICU · OT · critical care
- IPD wards, beds & admissions  
- Admission request / approval  
- Admission deposit  
- Bed allocation (concurrency-safe)  
- Bed movement / transfers  
- Nursing assessment & vitals  
- Doctor IPD rounds & orders  
- Interim IPD billing  
- Discharge clearance & discharge summary  
- Bed release  
- ICU module  
- OT / theatre module  
- LAMA / DAMA / death / MLC clinical events  

### 7.9 Billing, payments & insurance
- Encounter invoices  
- OPD billing  
- Manual payment recording  
- Packages & discounts  
- Receipts & refunds  
- Payment gateway (e.g. Razorpay)  
- Insurance / TPA — policies, pre-auth, claims  

### 7.10 Notifications & engagement
- In-app notifications (e.g. OPD, lab report ready)  
- Notification channel expansion (SMS / WhatsApp / push as product channels)  
- Follow-up reminders  

### 7.11 Analytics, timeline & insights
- Role dashboards  
- Reports & operational dashboards  
- Unified patient healthcare timeline  
- Health analytics / lab trends on patient journey  
- Management operational & financial visibility  

### 7.12 Security, staff & platform
- IAM (JWT)  
- RBAC — role-based access control  
- Staff invite & roles  
- Multi-role users (patient + staff roles)  
- Multi-tenant hospital SaaS (`tenant_id`)  
- Hospital / receptionist / doctor / patient portals  
- Hospital admin portal  
- Mobile HMS (nurse, reception, doctor IPD)  
- Security & compliance hardening  
- Performance & reliability  
- Enterprise UAT readiness  

### 7.13 Hospital Asset Management
- Biomedical, care-unit, facility & IT asset registry  
- Asset lifecycle (in stock → assigned → maintenance → retired)  
- Warranty / AMC tracking  
- Location linkage (ward / bed / OT / lab)  
- Biomedical & hospital admin portals for assets  

### 7.14 Ecosystem reach
- Independent laboratory organizations  
- Independent pharmacy organizations  
- Connected patient journey across hospitals, doctors, labs & pharmacies  
- Documents on the patient journey  

### 7.15 Connected journey (visual story for flyer)

Use this as the **ecosystem / journey visual** (thin-line, elegant — not a busy infographic):

```text
UHID → Appointment → Check-in → OPD Queue → Consultation
  → Diagnosis · e-Rx · Wellness
  → Laboratory → Pharmacy
  → IPD (as needed) → Billing → Follow-up · Timeline
```

## 11. Dimensions, print & QR

| Priority | Size | Orientation |
|----------|------|-------------|
| Primary | A4 — 210 × 297 mm | Portrait |
| Alternate | Letter — 8.5 × 11 in | Portrait |
| Handout | A5 — 148 × 210 mm | Portrait |
| Rack | DL — 99 × 210 mm | Portrait |

- Bleed 3 mm · safe margin ≥ 10 mm · 300 dpi · CMYK print + RGB digital PDF  
- QR ≥ 20 × 20 mm, dark on white quiet zone, never on busy photo without white pad  
- Matte / soft-touch preferred

## 12. Image & typography direction

- Premium healthcare-tech photography (clinician + patient or modern hospital digital care)  
- Calm, trustworthy, high-end corporate — **not** generic poster, cartoon, or sci-fi AI art  
- Modern geometric sans (Outfit / similar)  
- Large Health360 + headline; minimal body copy; no tiny text walls  
- Thin-line icons only if needed (few); elegant curves; restrained purple/navy

## 13. Designer DOs and DON’Ts

### DO
- Treat §7 items as **Health360 features** (no “coming soon” badges)  
- Keep Health360 hero; TechEarnest logo official and secondary  
- Use the recommended capability strip for the page; full catalog is reference  
- Strong CTA + scan-ready QR  
- Enterprise healthcare-tech look  

### DON’T
- Mark features as “future,” “roadmap,” “beta,” or “implemented only”  
- Invent awards, fake stats, hospital counts, or #1 claims  
- Distort the TechEarnest logo  
- Overload with 20 feature cards or dashboard screenshots  
- Mix this flyer with a consumer “Scan to join event” pamphlet CTA  
- Use cartoon / neon / AI-brain visuals
