Diagnostics & medicines

Lab and pharmacy journeys without paper handoffs

Laboratory Hospital lab journey end-to-end

Doctor orders → patient books hospital lab → unique specimen ID → process → verify → publish. Structured values feed health metrics. Patient gets “report ready” notify.

Pharmacy E-Rx to dispense & MAR

Signed digital prescriptions, send Rx to hospital or retail pharmacy, inventory-aware dispense, and inpatient MAR so medicines stay digital from doctor to bedside.

Radiology / OT Imaging & theatre

Clinical orders into radiology worklists; OT scheduling and procedure notes tied to the same encounter as the OPD or IPD visit.

Patient Consultation package

After the visit, patients see today’s diagnosis, medicines, wellness plan, lab orders, and follow-up in one clear package.

Catalogs Hospital clinical catalogs

Symptoms, diagnoses, and dosage templates — hospital-ready catalogs that speed structured documentation.

Continuity Follow-up that sticks

Follow-up dates become part of the clinical record and patient journey — ready for the next consult with full history.
