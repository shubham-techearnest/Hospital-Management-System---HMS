Patient journey

How care flows

Every step uses one UHID and one encounter hub — that is the Health360 architecture.

  1. 1
    Register & UHID
    Lifelong patient identity, profile, consent, and health timeline.
  2. 2
    Book · Arrive · Live OPD queue
    Appointments, desk or QR arrive, tokens, and visit status sync.
  3. 3
    Consult · Diagnose · E-prescribe
    Doctor workspace, catalogs, signed digital Rx, wellness, follow-up.
  4. 4
    Lab · Pharmacy · Imaging
    Hospital lab to report, send Rx to pharmacy, MAR, radiology & OT.
  5. 5
    IPD · Billing · AI · Assets
    Beds & ICU, invoices, health insights, biomedical & facility assets.
